# 往年 ELPA 题目与提示小问

[返回题库目录](./README.md) · [查看 2026 Fall 反馈](./2026-fall.md)

> 以下题目来自既有《ELPA 考试流程与题库》及考生分享资料，属于**往年流传题库／考生回忆整理**，不是学校官方公布或核验的题库。原资料同时收集了几组相似问法，已在相关题目下合并呈现；具体考试题卡不一定包含全部小问。

## Q01｜Undergraduate study

**Main question:** Describe and reflect upon your experience of undergraduate study.  
**中文：** 描述并反思你的本科学习经历。

**收集到的提示小问：**
- What was your degree / major? — 本科学位／专业是什么？
- Why did you study this subject? — 为什么选择这个专业？
- What do you think about the courses you took? — 如何评价学过的课程？
- What course did you enjoy the most as an undergraduate? Why? — 最喜欢哪门课？为什么？
- What is one thing you would change in your courses if you could? — 如果可以，想改变课程的哪一方面？
- What is one thing you would keep in the course? — 哪些方面值得保留？
- If you were going to study as an undergraduate again, what would you do differently? — 假如重新读本科，会作出什么不同选择？

*同类反馈还提到上过哪些课、最不喜欢哪门课及原因。*

## Q02｜Explain your research clearly

**Main question:** Describe your research using clear and simple language. If you use complex terminology, be sure to explain it so that others can understand.  
**中文：** 用清晰、容易理解的语言说明研究内容，并解释专业术语。

**收集到的提示小问：**
- What is the general topic of your research? — 研究主题是什么？
- What is your research project about? — 项目研究什么？
- Why are you interested in this area of research? — 为什么对这个领域感兴趣？
- Why is research in your area important to society? — 研究为什么具有社会意义？
- Can you give an example of a real-world application of your research? — 举一个真实应用场景。
- What main challenges have you faced in your research so far? — 有哪些主要困难？
- What do you hope to achieve in your research? — 希望达成什么目标？

*同类反馈还包括研究问题、课题细节及前景。*

## Q03｜English-speaking event as a postgraduate

**Main question:** Describe an event (or situation) where you were required to speak in English as a research postgraduate.  
**中文：** 介绍一次作为研究型研究生需要使用英语交流或发言的场合。

**收集到的提示小问：**
- What was the event and where did it take place? — 这是什么活动，在哪里？
- How long was / will it be? — 活动持续多久？
- Why did / will you attend it? — 为什么参加？
- Were / will you be able to prepare in advance? If so, how? — 能否提前准备，如何准备？
- Who were / will be the other people present? — 还有谁参加？
- What did / will you talk about? — 你会谈些什么？
- Were you successful? Why or why not? — 表现如何，为什么？
- Did / will the event involve conversation or exchange of information, or mainly individual speaking? — 主要是互动交流还是个人陈述？

*此题在后续补充资料中亦有相似反馈；并非独立新增题种。*

## Q04｜Presenting research in English

**Main question:** Describe your experience of speaking or presenting your research in English.  
**中文：** 介绍用英语谈论或展示学术研究的经历。

**收集到的提示小问：**
- When did you speak about your research or academic interests? — 何时谈过研究或学术兴趣？
- Have you had many opportunities to present your research in English? — 有多少相关经历？
- If not, have you had opportunities to speak about academic topics in English? — 是否谈过其他学术话题？
- What is most challenging about presenting research in English? — 最大困难是什么？
- What do you like about presenting research in English? — 喜欢哪些方面？
- What would you like to improve about your English presentation skills? — 希望提高哪些表达能力？

*同类反馈还涉及何时、向谁、在哪里发言等信息。*

## Q05｜Why HKUST?

**Main question:** Describe why you chose to study at HKUST.  
**中文：** 说明选择到港科大学习的原因。

**收集到的提示小问：**
- What did you hear about HKUST before you came here? — 来校之前对学校有哪些了解？
- What attracted you to study at HKUST? — 哪些地方吸引了你？
- What were your first impressions of the campus? — 校园的第一印象如何？
- How does HKUST compare with your undergraduate university? — 与本科院校有何不同？
- What do you know about your research department at HKUST? — 对研究方向或院系有何了解？
- Which professors will you be working with? — 计划与哪位老师合作？
- Aside from studies, what else do you hope to achieve at HKUST? — 学业之外想收获什么？

*注意：这组来源使用“HKUST”的原有写法；不要擅自把所有原题替换成“HKUST(GZ)”。2026 年校园体验相关反馈另见[2026 Fall](./2026-fall.md)。*

## Q06｜A study or work challenge

**Main question:** Describe a challenge you faced in your studies or at work. How did you overcome it?  
**中文：** 介绍学习或工作中遇到的挑战以及应对方法。

**收集到的提示小问：**
- What was the challenge? — 遇到了什么困难？
- Why was it a challenge? — 为什么它是困难？
- What did you do to overcome it? — 采取了哪些措施？
- Did you handle it well? Why? — 是否解决得好，为什么？
- What did you learn from this challenge? — 学到了什么？
- Would you deal with it differently now? — 现在会有不同做法吗？

## Q07｜An interview experience

**Main question:** Describe a time when you attended an interview (e.g., job, university, etc.).  
**中文：** 描述一次参加面试的经历。

**收集到的提示小问：**
- What type of interview did you attend? — 参加的是什么面试？
- Did you do well? Why? — 表现如何？为什么？
- What did you learn from this interview? — 有什么收获？
- Do you think presentation skills are important for interviews? Why? — 面试表达能力重要吗？为什么？
- How can these skills be improved? — 如何提高？
- Should universities teach students such skills? — 大学是否应该教授相关技能？

**中文原记录：** 描述一下你在互联网中能学到什么？

- 以上问题的英文主句尽可能沿用既有资料；部分提示小问经过轻微语法与格式规范化，**并非保证与现场题卡逐字相同**。
